What you'll learn

A system you can use week after week.

Over five days, I’ll show you how to take the overwhelm out of meal prep by preparing a few useful basics ahead of time, then using them in different ways throughout the week. The goal isn’t to prep every meal — it’s to make eating well feel easier.

Prep the basics

Use my simple 2 + 2 + 3 method to prepare useful proteins, carbohydrates and vegetables for the week ahead.

Mix & match

Turn the same prepped ingredients into different balanced meals, so you're not eating the same thing every day.

Have a back-up plan

Keep smart fridge, freezer and pantry staples on hand so you can still put a good meal together when life gets busy.

Meal prep tools & tricks

Learn which storage and kitchen tools can make meal prep easier, from choosing the right containers to storing your prepped food safely and knowing how long it keeps in the fridge.
